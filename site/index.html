Post one short video everywhere, without retyping everything

ShortPost is a free, open-source Windows app for creators. Drop one vertical video into a folder. ShortPost writes a separate title, description and hashtags for each platform with AI, shows you everything for approval, then schedules or posts it for you.

What it does

  • Writes different text per platform (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky) from your video's file name and notes.
  • Shows a preview of every post. Nothing is posted until you approve it.
  • Picks the next free day, so you keep a steady one-video-a-day rhythm.
  • Posts to TikTok with the official TikTok Content Posting API: you sign in with your own TikTok account, choose the privacy level, comment/duet/stitch settings and any content disclosure in the app, and ShortPost only uploads when you confirm.

How it works

ShortPost runs on your own Windows PC. Your videos, drafts and sign-in tokens stay on your computer. You connect each platform with your own account; ShortPost never sees your passwords.

Requirements

Windows 10 or 11, Python 3.12 (installed by the setup script), FFmpeg, and either a free Google Gemini API key or a local Ollama model for the AI text.
